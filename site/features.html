Feature matrix

Everything it does, and what changes if you bring your own.

AstroDeck is a complete observatory controller on its own. It can also sit on top of a NINA or ASIAIR setup you already like. This page says what each route gives you.

Why two ASIAIR cells say "not yet". The filter wheel and rotator are the two commands that could not be mapped to certainty without a box on the bench. A wrong guess there rotates to the wrong angle or images through the wrong filter, silently, for a whole night. So AstroDeck declines to guess.

Setting expectations

What is not built, or not proven.

  • No flats wizard. Flat auto-exposure and a calibration library exist; the guided walkthrough does not.
  • The ASIAIR backend has never touched real hardware. Written against the protocol, tested against a fake. It needs pip install -e .[asiair] and is absent if you skip it.
  • The satellite cloud dome is the Americas only. GOES footprint. Forecast, safety monitor and frame-based cloud detection are global.
  • Guiding and plate solving have sharp corners. Used every session, and both can still fail with a message that could be clearer.
  • Binaries are not code-signed. Windows and macOS will warn on first run.
