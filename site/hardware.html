Hardware

Bring the gear you already own.

Three ways in: a native driver with nothing else installed, ASCOM Alpaca over the network, or a Windows ASCOM driver through the COM host AstroDeck starts for you. Most rigs need only the first.

Native drivers

No vendor software. Nothing to install.

AstroDeck talks to these directly — over the vendor SDK or the wire protocol itself. Plug in the USB cable and press connect.

Everything else

If it speaks ASCOM, it works.

Direct

ASCOM Alpaca

Any Alpaca endpoint connects over the network with no bridge at all — cameras, mounts, focusers, wheels, rotators, domes, safety monitors, switches and weather stations. Discovery finds them; you pick roles. ZWO, Pegasus Astro, QHY, PrimaLuceLab, Optec, Lakeside, Moonlite and the rest of the ASCOM ecosystem all reach AstroDeck this way.

Windows-only drivers

The bundled COM host

Gear that only ships a Windows COM driver still works: AstroDeck starts and manages a local COM-to-Alpaca host itself. No separate ASCOM Remote install, no second thing to keep running, and it is simply absent on Linux and macOS rather than half-present.

Not on the list?

Ask. Genuinely.

The list above is what has been built so far, not a judgement about what deserves to exist. If your gear is missing, open an issue with what you have and how it connects.

For you

Open an issue

Tell us the vendor, the model, and whether it has an ASCOM driver, an Alpaca endpoint or a documented serial protocol. That is usually enough to know how much work it is.

For a contributor

A handful of async methods

A backend implements a small protocol against devices/base.py. There is a contract test suite every backend runs against, so "does it behave like a mount" is a question with an automated answer.

For a vendor

No fork required

Backends can ship as separate packages and register themselves through an entry point. Your driver, your repo, your release cadence — it just appears in the equipment list.
