11 Running the Night

Sequencing + safety

A sequence runs the whole night unattended — acquire, focus, dithered subs, filter swaps — then parks at dawn.

Meridian flip
On a German equatorial, when the target crosses the meridian (the overhead line) the tube must swing to the mount's other side, then re-solve, re-center, re-calibrate.
Dithering
Nudge a few pixels between subs so fixed sensor specks don't stack into streaks.
Safety monitor
Watches cloud, dawn, dome close — pauses or aborts on unsafe.
Hard rule
Never slew at the Sun. Park the mount at the end.
safe · imaging

Miss the flip and the scope drives into the tripod, or tracking reverses and every sub after it trails — the second half of the night is scrap.

AstroDeck's sequencer chains the night; the safety monitor pauses on unsafe and the run ends at park.

Software that babysits a robot pointing a glass tube at the sky, all night, alone.
