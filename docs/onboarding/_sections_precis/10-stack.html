10 Stacking & Calibration

Exposure + calibration

One sub is mostly noise. Stack many and the random grain averages away while real detail accumulates.

Why stack
Noise drops as ~√N — 4× the subs halves it, 100× cuts it tenfold.
Integration
Sub length × count. 5min × 60 = 5h total.
Darks
Lens capped (no light), same exposure + temp — maps the sensor's thermal pattern (dark current, amp glow, hot pixels).
Flats
Even-lit blank field — divides out dust shadows + vignetting.
Bias / dark-flats
Very short, capped frames — capture the sensor's fixed bias offset / read pattern.

Skip calibration and the fixed errors survive stacking: amp glow, dust donuts, and vignetting bake straight into the final image.

AstroDeck's sequence settings set sub length and count, and the calibration plan schedules darks/flats or pulls a matched library.

It is averaging your way out of a bad photo, sixty times over.
