05 Quieting the Sensor

Sensor cooling

A thermoelectric cooler holds the sensor at a fixed cold temperature, keeping dark-current noise low and calibration frames repeatable.

Noise source
Dark current — self-generated; roughly doubles every ~6°C, grows with exposure time.
Set-point
A stable target like -10°C; lower and steady beats merely cold.
Floor
TEC reaches roughly 30-40°C below ambient.
Ramp
Cool and warm gradually to avoid condensation and thermal shock.
COOLINGLOCKED -10°

Grain clears as the sensor falls to its set-point and the cooler locks.

Drift the temperature between subs and your library of dark frames no longer matches, so calibration leaves noise it was meant to subtract.

AstroDeck's cooler box shows target vs. actual with a cooling-state indicator that reads LOCKED once it holds.

It is a tiny fridge bolted to a camera, sulking at physics.
