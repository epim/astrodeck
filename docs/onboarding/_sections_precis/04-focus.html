04 Getting Sharp

Focusing

In focus = the tightest star dot, judged by a number (HFR / FWHM, smaller is sharper), never by eye.

Metric
HFR / FWHM — the star's width in pixels. Smaller = sharper.
Drift
As the night cools, metal contracts and focus wanders — refocus periodically.
Bahtinov
A mask throws 3 diffraction spikes; the center spike sits dead-center when focused.
V-curve
Autofocus steps the focuser, plots HFR vs position, and drives to the minimum.
blur → Bahtinov lock
V-curve autofocus

Soft focus is unrecoverable — bloated stars and lost faint detail bake into every frame and no later step sharpens them back.
