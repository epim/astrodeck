03 Aiming the Axis

Polar alignment

Pointing the mount's spin axis at the celestial pole (near Polaris, north) so tracking actually cancels the drift.

Target
The celestial pole — within a degree of Polaris in the north.
Goal
Dial alt/az to a bullseye until error < ~30 arcsec.
Method
A routine plate-solves the sky to measure the error; you turn two knobs to zero it.
misaligned → aligned
bullseye error

Left: an arc means error — the pinpoint is what aligned looks like. Right: nudge the dot into the center ring.

Error rotates the whole field around the pole; stars smear into arcs that no later processing can salvage.

AstroDeck's polar bullseye screen: an amber error-dot, rings that tighten as the error drops, and live alt/az nudge cues until you hit center.

You are aiming at a hinge you cannot see, by adjusting two screws you can barely reach.
