01 The Hardware

The rig

The imaging train: a chain of devices that aim, hold focus, and record light, bolted nose to tail.

Core 3
Mount (aims + tracks), OTA (the telescope, i.e. the lens), main camera (the sensor).
Helpers
Guide scope + guide camera, motorized focuser, filter wheel, sometimes a rotator.
Count
4–7 separate boxes on a typical setup, each its own driver.

Amber = the core three. The rest are helpers that earn their place on a long night.

One loose connection or a flexing joint and every frame in the train inherits the wobble.

AstroDeck drives all of them from one control surface — one screen, one connection list, every box.

Six gadgets, one cable nest, and you, in the dark, hoping none of them blink out.
