08 Holding the Star

Guiding + dithering

A second camera watches one star and pulse-corrects the mount many times a minute to hold a long exposure steady.

Why drift
Imperfect gears plus atmosphere pull tracking off over minutes.
Rate
Corrections fire many times per minute from the guide frames.
Target
Hold error to < 1″ RMS for clean stars.
Dither
Shift a few pixels between subs so stacking rejects hot pixels and patterns.
GUIDE STAR
RMS ERROR 0.6"

The star jitters and gets nudged back to center; the error trace rides near zero, then a dither hop offsets the next sub.

Let the error climb past an arcsecond and round stars stretch into little commas across every sub in the stack.

AstroDeck's guiding graph plots live RA/Dec error and RMS, with one-click dither between subs.

A robot playing the world's slowest game of keep-the-dot-centered.
