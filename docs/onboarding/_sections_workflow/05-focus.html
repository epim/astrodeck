05 Focusing

Find focus

Move the focuser until stars are as tight as possible — measured by the software, not eyeballed.

HFR
Focus quality is the star's half-flux radius; smaller is sharper.
V-curve
HFR vs focuser position traces a V; autofocus samples both arms and solves for the minimum.
Per filter
Each filter comes to focus at a slightly different point (filter focus offsets).
Temp drift
Focus shifts as the scope cools, so it refocuses on a temperature delta.

Autofocus samples the V and drives the focuser to its floor.

Soft focus fattens every star and drowns faint detail — no amount of processing puts it back.

FocusView shows a Live Preview, an Autofocus routine (exposure, step size) and a V-Curve · HFR vs Position plot; Automation can autofocus every N frames, apply filter offsets, and refocus on a temperature delta.
