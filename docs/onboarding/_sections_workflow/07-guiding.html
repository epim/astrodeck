07 Guiding

Guide out the drift (PHD2)

A second camera watches one star and sends tiny correction pulses to the mount, cancelling the small errors that tracking and polar alignment leave behind.

Loop
Measure the guide star's offset, pulse the mount to push it back, repeat every ~1–3 s.
RMS
Guiding quality is the residual error in arcseconds; smaller means rounder stars.
Dither
Shift a few pixels between frames so stacking rejects hot pixels and walking noise.
PHD2
The de-facto guiding engine AstroDeck drives.

Each pulse pushes the star back toward the crosshair; the trace is the residual it can't catch.

Without guiding, the field slowly walks during long sub-exposures and stars smear into ovals.

AstroDeck reports live guide RMS and exposes a dither setting in the sequence Automation.
