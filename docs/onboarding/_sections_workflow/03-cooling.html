03 Cooling

Cool the camera

Run the camera's thermoelectric cooler down to a stable set-point so thermal noise stays low and, just as important, repeatable.

Ambient
Uncooled, the sensor sits near ambient (sim: +12.3 °C).
Set-point
A typical target is −10 °C, held to a fixed delta all night.
Dark current
Sensor noise roughly halves for every ~6 °C cooler.
Headroom
Pick a set-point the cooler can hold (~70–80% power) so it stays locked as the night warms or cools.

Drag the set-point — the grain is dark current you can't fully subtract away.

A warm sensor buries faint nebulosity in thermal noise; cooling too far or too fast can frost the sensor with condensation.

The cooler holds the set-point to within ±1 °C and can warm gently at dawn — but there's no automatic frost guard, so pick a set-point the cooler can actually hold.

AstroDeck's monitor shows live sensor temperature and cooler power %, and the sequence can warm the cooler gently at the end of the night (warm_cooler_when_done).

−10 °C isn't sacred; it just has to be repeatable, so your dark frames match.
