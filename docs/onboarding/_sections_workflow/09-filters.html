09 Filter Wheel

Filters, and how they combine

A mono camera shoots through one filter at a time; you stack each channel separately, then combine them into a color image.

Wheel
A motorized wheel rotates filters in front of the sensor (sim: a 7×36 mm wheel).
Broadband
L R G B: red/green/blue build natural color, and luminance adds brightness and detail.
Narrowband
Ha OIII SII isolate single emission lines; they reject light pollution and moonlight but pass little light, so subs run long.
False color
Narrowband channels are assigned to RGB by choice; the "Hubble palette" is SHO (SII→R, Ha→G, OIII→B).

Toggle channels — RGB build natural color and L adds the punch; the SHO trio each lights different structure into a false-color palette.

Forget to refocus per filter or to match each filter's exposure to its throughput and a channel comes back soft or starved — the composite is unusable.

Per-filter focus offsets shift focus automatically on each change, HFR-spike rejection flags bad frames, and exposure and gain are set per filter in the plan.

AstroDeck moves the wheel by slot (/api/filterwheel/position), names the slots L R G B Ha OIII SII, and applies per-filter focus offsets in Automation.

The colors of a narrowband image are a decision, not a measurement.
