04 First Slew

Slew to a bright star

Send the mount to a known bright star — often Polaris or another northern object — to check pointing and give focus something to work on.

GoTo
The mount slews using its internal pointing model.
The miss
An uncalibrated model lands the star off-centre, sometimes outside the frame entirely.
Plate-solve + sync
Image the field, solve where the scope actually points, and sync the model to truth.
Park
A known home reference (the sim parks near the pole at Dec +89.5°).

First GoTo lands close, not centred; the plate-solve fixes the rest.

Trust an unsynced model and the target sits outside the sensor — you expose empty sky and never notice.

MountView has a Slew Pad, a Pointing panel (RA / Dec / Alt / Az / State) and a Target Catalog; a plate-solve syncs the model to where it truly points.

The mount is confident and wrong; the camera is how it learns.
